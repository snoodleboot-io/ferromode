================================================================================
                        TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

LSTM Architecture:
  Input:     20 samples (normalized [-1, 1])
  LSTM L1:   128 hidden units + tanh + dropout(0.2)
  LSTM L2:   128 hidden units + tanh + dropout(0.2)
  Output:    10 samples (tanh bounded)

Model Selection:
  Stationarity Score > 0.7 → AR model (fast, stationary)
  Stationarity Score ≤ 0.7 → LSTM model (adaptive, non-stationary)

Training Data:
  Pure tones:        10% (simple oscillation)
  Chirps:            15% (frequency sweep)
  AM/FM modulated:   15% (envelope modulation)
  Non-stationary:    20% (time-varying)
  Intermittent:      20% (burst signals)
  Real-world-like:   20% (mixed, noisy)

Performance Targets:
  LSTM inference:    < 1 ms
  AR inference:      < 0.1 ms
  Model size:        < 2 MB (FP16)
  End-effect improvement: > 30% vs AR
  Test MSE:          < 0.05
